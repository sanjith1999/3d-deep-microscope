\section{Reconstruction and validation}
\label{sec:s:reconstruction}

\paragraph{Shared framework.}
The encoder changes the forward operator; the prior and solver family remain fixed across encoders. TV uses $\tfrac12\|\bA\bx-\by\|_2^2+\lambda\|\nabla\bx\|_{2,1}$ with a box constraint. PnP uses the quadratic data update and denoising/sparsity branches described in the main paper. A quadratic data term penalizes residuals but does not guarantee membership in the hard measurement-interval set used in \cref{sec:s:uncertainty}. Generic denoisers need not minimize an explicit regularization energy.

\paragraph{TV.}
We use isotropic 3D forward-difference TV and ADMM with separate gradient and box branches, both with unit penalty. There are 300 ADMM iterations; each conjugate-gradient volume update uses at most 20 iterations at relative tolerance $10^{-5}$. The starting volume is normalized back-projection. The regularization weight is selected from $\{0.01,0.03,0.1,0.3,1,3\}$ using mean validation PSNR. Edge selections extend the grid by a factor of three, at most twice. These fixed iteration budgets are not claims of exact minimization. Additional checks with 1000 iterations changed mean PSNR by $-0.03$\,dB at $C=8$ and centroid error by at most $0.001\um$; checks at $C=1$ changed PSNR by at most $0.03$\,dB and centroid error by $0.006\um$.

\paragraph{PnP.}
Both variants run 20 ADMM iterations, with at most 20 conjugate-gradient steps per volume update and relative tolerance $10^{-5}$. Denoising-branch penalties are $5s$ and the sparsity penalty is $10s$. The implementation sets nominal branch weights of 0.2 for the slice denoisers and 1.0 for the volumetric denoiser. The fixed denoisers ignore this proximal-scale argument, so these values activate the branches rather than adjusting denoising strength. Their coupling to the data is controlled by the shared penalty. The sparsity weight is $0.2l$; branches with zero weight are omitted. Validation selects $l=0$ for all PnP settings in the main 60-crop comparison; sparsity is active only in some supplementary conditions. We validate $s\in\{2^{-4},2^{-3},\ldots,4\}$ and $l\in\{0,1/16\}$. Boundary selections extend the scale grid by a factor of two and the nonzero sparsity scale by four, at most twice. Final selections are interior. All PnP results start from normalized back-projection and are clipped to $[0,1]$ only after the final iteration.

\paragraph{Denoisers.}
Pretrained PnP uses SCICO's 17-layer natural-image DnCNN \cite{zhang2017,balke2022scico}, independently along three slice orientations. Its denoising noise level is fixed; it does not receive a specimen-dependent noise estimate. Neuron-trained PnP replaces this 2D model and adds a 3D branch. The 2D network has 17 layers and 64 channels; the 3D network has 12 layers and 48 channels. We do not evaluate 2D-only and 3D-only neuron-trained variants, so the comparison changes both the training distribution and denoising architecture.

Training uses 459 crops from cell 1, stacks 8 and 9 of animal SOM009, with stack 10 held out for monitoring. Duplicated planes and planes whose mean falls below half their neighbors' median are excluded before crop extraction. Both networks use Adam at $10^{-3}$, Gaussian noise $\sigma\sim\mathcal U(0,0.25)$, flips, lateral transpositions and intensity scaling in $[0.3,1]$. The 2D model uses random slices for 30,000 steps at batch size 64; the 3D model uses volumes for 15,000 steps at batch size 8. Neither contributes test data or is updated during optical training or reconstruction.

\paragraph{Parameter selection.}
The two vascular validation crops come from another animal and are used for all neuronal comparisons. Parameters are selected separately by encoder, compression and changed noise condition, with the same selection rule and fixed prior weights. No neuronal test crop is used for tuning. Optical design uses neither validation nor test specimens. The pipeline may receive a reference array for reporting metrics, but the reference does not enter the reconstruction updates.
