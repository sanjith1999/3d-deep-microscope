\section{Reconstruction and validation}
\label{sec:s:reconstruction}

\paragraph{Shared framework.}
For TV and PnP, we change the operator and validation-selected scalar parameters across encoders, while keeping denoiser weights and solver families fixed. \Cref{sec:s:joint} specifies the separately fine-tuned feed-forward decoder. TV minimizes $\tfrac12\|\bA\bx-\by\|_2^2+\lambda\|\nabla\bx\|_{2,1}$ with a box constraint. PnP alternates a quadratic data update with denoising and optional sparsity branches. A quadratic penalty does not enforce the hard measurement intervals in \cref{sec:s:uncertainty}, and a generic denoiser need not minimize a known regularization energy.

\paragraph{ADMM and plug-and-play updates.}
Let $C_j$ map the volume to branch $j$, with auxiliary variable $\mathbf v_j$ and scaled dual $\mathbf d_j$. Each iteration computes
\begin{align}
\bx^{k+1}&=\arg\min_{\bx}\;\Bigl\{\tfrac12\|\bA\bx-\by\|_2^2\notag\\
&\qquad{}+\sum_j\tfrac{\rho_j}{2}\|C_j\bx-\mathbf v_j^k+\mathbf d_j^k\|_2^2\Bigr\},\\
\mathbf v_j^{k+1}&=\mathcal F_j(C_j\bx^{k+1}+\mathbf d_j^k),\\
\mathbf d_j^{k+1}&=\mathbf d_j^k+C_j\bx^{k+1}-\mathbf v_j^{k+1}.
\end{align}
We solve the data update by conjugate gradients. For TV, $C_j$ is the 3D gradient or identity, and $\mathcal F_j$ applies vector soft thresholding or box projection. PnP uses identity branch maps and replaces proximal functions with denoisers. If $P_o$ rearranges the volume into slices of orientation $o\in\{xy,yz,zx\}$, a slice branch applies $P_o^{-1}DP_o$, with $D$ denoising each slice independently. Pretrained PnP uses all three orientations; neuron-trained PnP replaces their shared 2D network and adds a 3D branch. The optional $\ell_1$ branch applies scalar soft thresholding.

\paragraph{TV.}
We use isotropic 3D forward-difference TV with separate gradient and box ADMM branches, both at unit penalty. The box branch projects its auxiliary variable each iteration; we clip the final estimate to $[0,1]$. We start from normalized, clipped back-projection and run 300 ADMM iterations, with at most 20 conjugate-gradient steps per update at relative tolerance $10^{-5}$. We select the regularization weight from $\{0.01,0.03,0.1,0.3,1,3\}$ by mean validation PSNR. If validation selects an endpoint, we extend the grid by a factor of three, at most twice. All five primary designs select $\lambda=0.1$ at $C=8$. A fixed budget does not establish exact minimization: 1000-iteration checks changed mean PSNR by $-0.03$\,dB at $C=8$ and centroid error by at most $0.001\um$; at $C=1$, changes were at most $0.03$\,dB and $0.006\um$.

\paragraph{PnP.}
Both PnP variants run 20 ADMM iterations, with at most 20 conjugate-gradient steps per update and relative tolerance $10^{-5}$. Denoising-branch penalties are $5s$ and the sparsity penalty is $10s$. Nominal branch weights are 0.2 for slice denoisers and 1.0 for the 3D denoiser. The fixed networks ignore the proximal-scale argument, so these weights activate branches rather than set denoising strength; the shared penalty controls coupling to the data. The sparsity weight is $0.2l$, and we omit zero-weight branches. At $C=8$, pretrained PnP uses $s=0.25,l=0$ for every design. Neuron-trained PnP uses $s=0.125$ for the untrained plate and $s=0.25$ for learned designs; $l=1/16$ only for seed-0 Fisher and zero otherwise. We validate $s\in\{2^{-4},2^{-3},\ldots,4\}$ and $l\in\{0,1/16\}$. Boundary selections extend the scale grid by two and the nonzero sparsity scale by four, at most twice; all final selections are interior. We initialize by normalized back-projection and clip to $[0,1]$ only after the final iteration.

\paragraph{Denoisers.}
Pretrained PnP applies SCICO's 17M natural-image DnCNN independently along the three slice orientations \cite{zhang2017,balke2022scico}. It has 17 layers, was trained at noise standard deviation 0.1, and receives no specimen-dependent noise estimate. Neuron-trained PnP uses a 17-layer, 64-channel 2D network plus a 12-layer, 48-channel 3D network. We do not test 2D-only or 3D-only neuronal variants, so this comparison changes both training distribution and denoising architecture.

We train on 459 crops from SOM009 cell 1, stacks 8 and 9, holding out stack 10 for monitoring. Before extracting crops, we exclude duplicate planes and planes whose mean is below half their neighbors' median. Both networks use Adam at $10^{-3}$, Gaussian noise $\sigma\sim\mathcal U(0,0.25)$, flips, lateral transpositions and intensity scaling in $[0.3,1]$. The 2D network trains on random slices for 30,000 steps at batch size 64; the 3D network trains on volumes for 15,000 steps at batch size 8. Neither uses test data, and we keep their weights fixed during optical training and reconstruction.

\paragraph{Parameter selection.}
We select scalar parameters on two vascular crops from another animal, separately for each encoder, compression and changed noise condition. We keep the selection rule and prior weights fixed, and never tune on neuronal test crops. Model-only optical design uses no specimens. The supervised reference selects checkpoints on its held-out neuronal volumes (\cref{sec:s:joint}). Reconstruction updates do not use reference arrays; we use them only to report metrics.
