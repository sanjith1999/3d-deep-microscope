\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We learn a defocused phase plate by decorrelating detector-integrated depth signatures, without specimen examples or a reconstruction decoder. In fixed-focus fluorescence simulations at eightfold measurement compression and matched nominal dose, our plate achieves 10--26\,\% lower axial-profile W1 than worst-case coherence and Fisher-information designs across four reconstruction methods. Controlled crossings also show improved filament separation over the untrained plate under TV and pretrained PnP. Mean errors under iterative reconstruction are close to those of the end-to-end plate, which performs better with its own decoder. These results show that depth discrimination can guide optical design before a reconstruction method is chosen.
