\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We learn depth-encoding illumination from the optical model alone, without specimen examples or a reconstruction decoder. In fixed-focus fluorescence simulations, the same phase plate improves axial-profile recovery across four reconstruction methods and gives competitive results against a jointly trained plate. Joint training retains an advantage with its own decoder, while the model-only criterion improves the tested low-dose crossing task. These results show how targeting physical depth responses can improve axial recovery while leaving reconstruction choice outside optical training.
