\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We demonstrate decoder-free optical learning for fixed-focus fluorescence microscopy by optimizing depth-dependent measurement signatures. The resulting phase plate improves axial-profile recovery across TV and two PnP methods, reducing W1 by 28--50\,\% and centroid error by 30--54\,\% in neuronal simulations. Controlled crossings show smaller recovered separations with two of the priors. These results support learning the excitation measurements as a way to improve axial recovery without tailoring the optical design to a reconstruction network.
