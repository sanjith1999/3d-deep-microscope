\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We learn depth-encoding illumination from the optical model alone, without specimen examples or a reconstruction decoder. In fixed-focus fluorescence simulations, the same plate lowers mean axial-profile W1 from $0.27$--$0.58\um$ to $0.20$--$0.29\um$ across four reconstruction methods at eightfold measurement compression. It also separates crossing filaments at smaller gaps under two of the three iterative methods. Mean W1 differs from the end-to-end plate by less than $0.01\um$ under the iterative methods; the end-to-end system gives lower error with its own decoder. Depth signatures can therefore guide optical learning while leaving reconstruction choice outside the training loop.
