\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We learn a fixed phase plate for angle-scanned, fixed-focus fluorescence imaging by optimizing detector-integrated depth signatures, without specimen examples or a reconstruction solver. In simulations, the learned plate reduces axial-profile error by 28--50\,\% and centroid error by 30--54\,\% across TV and two PnP methods. Controlled crossings show improved separation under two of the three priors. These results demonstrate that optical learning can improve axial recovery across reconstruction methods while leaving the reconstruction prior outside the training loop.
