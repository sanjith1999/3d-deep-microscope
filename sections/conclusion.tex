\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We learn fixed-focus fluorescence illumination by optimising depth-dependent measurement signatures through the optical model. Training uses no specimen examples or reconstruction decoder. The learned plate reduces axial-profile error by 28--50\,\% across TV and two PnP methods, improves tested crossing separations under two priors, and achieves lower profile error at 0.3 times the random plate's excitation dose. These simulation results support depth-signature design as a route to improved volumetric recovery across reconstruction priors.
