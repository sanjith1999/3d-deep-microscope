\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A phase plate scanned in angle before an intermediate image plane gives each depth its own excitation sequence, and a specimen-free objective on the resulting measurement signatures improves that encoding without a decoder in the loop. In simulation, the learned plate lowered the depth error of TV and two plug-and-play decoders by 30--54\,\%, pointed the same way for a diffusion decoder, and narrowed calibrated depth intervals by 27\,\%, while an in-focus encoder left every tested decoder without depth. Learned optics can be trained for what the measurements record and then tested across decoders, rather than being matched to one.
