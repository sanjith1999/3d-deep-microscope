\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Depth-dependent illumination supplies cues for recovering overlapping fluorescence without axial refocusing. We study how to learn the phase profile that generates those cues in an angle-controlled system. In simulations, our model-only criterion lowers W1 by 10--26\,\% against the tested Fisher and coherence criteria across four reconstruction methods. Mean W1 under iterative reconstruction is within $0.01\um$ of that for the end-to-end plate, which performs better with its own network. Crossing separation improves over the untrained plate under two of three iterative methods. These results support designing the illumination from modeled voxel responses, while assessing both recovered depth profiles and distinct-structure separation.
