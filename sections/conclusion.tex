\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We learn a fixed phase plate for angle-scanned, fixed-focus fluorescence imaging from detector-integrated depth signatures. In simulations, it reduces axial-profile error by 28--50\,\% and centroid error by 30--54\,\% relative to its random initialization across TV and two PnP methods. Controlled crossings show improved separation under two priors. The results demonstrate that optical learning can improve axial recovery across the tested reconstruction methods without including them in training.
