\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We learn depth-encoding illumination for fixed-focus three-dimensional fluorescence microscopy by optimising a fixed phase plate through the optical forward model. Training reduces correlations between detector-integrated depth signatures without specimen examples or a reconstruction decoder. In simulations, the learned measurements better distinguish selected axial alternatives, and TV and pretrained PnP separate crossing filaments at a smaller tested gap. Axial-profile W1 decreases by 28--50\,\% across three reconstruction methods at eightfold measurement compression and equal deposited excitation dose. The benefits also persist at reduced dose. These results support learning the excitation as a way to improve depth discrimination and volumetric recovery across the tested reconstruction priors.
