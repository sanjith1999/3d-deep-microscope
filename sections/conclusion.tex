\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We learn a fixed phase plate for depth-encoding excitation through the optical forward model, without specimen examples or a reconstruction decoder. In simulations, the learned measurements better distinguish selected axial alternatives. TV and pretrained PnP separate crossing filaments at a smaller tested gap, while axial-profile W1 falls by 28--50\,\% across three reconstruction methods at eightfold measurement compression and equal deposited dose. Gains persist at reduced dose. Learning the excitation improves depth discrimination and recovery across the tested reconstruction priors.
