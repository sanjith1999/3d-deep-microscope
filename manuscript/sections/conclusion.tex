\section{Conclusion}

We presented a numerical framework for fixed-focus volumetric fluorescence recovery using angle-scanned phase illumination and multi-slice PnP. Fine-grid optical simulation and voxel integration reduce discretisation-induced model mismatch, while an analytical illumination--emission factorisation makes the workflow computationally feasible. Reconstruction reuses a pretrained two-dimensional denoiser without task-specific three-dimensional network training.

Dominant dendritic structures and their depth ordering remain recognisable in representative reconstructions at eight-fold measurement compression. The eight-subvolume test set quantifies the fidelity trade-off, while ablations show the benefits of depth-distinguishing illumination and voxel-integrated modelling. The framework supports volumetric recovery without axial specimen scanning or detection refocusing. Hardware validation, learned phase profiles and task-specific three-dimensional denoisers for potentially stronger compression remain future work.
